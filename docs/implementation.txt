CLIENT CONFIRMATION QUESTIONS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Use these before locking Phase 8.3+ and production scope.

Deployment & access
-------------------
1. Production URL: fixed domain with Let's Encrypt, or IP + self-signed (caregiver must accept cert)?
2. Who may log in — single nursery account, multiple caregivers, or parent home use?
3. Is one active monitoring session per server enough, or do you need multiple rooms/cameras at once?

Child & session setup (Phase 8.3)
---------------------------------
4. What devices/locations should appear in the picker (e.g. “Nursery Room A”, “Home kitchen”, T40 camera IDs)?
5. Is body temperature manual entry required for v1, or defer until T40 integration?
6. Should each meal session require selecting child + device + location, or remember last used?

Allergens & food
----------------
7. Is the current Big-9 + custom allergen list sufficient, or do you need a regulated allergen database / locale (EU 14, US FALCPA)?
8. For missed food labels: acceptable to rely on Clarifai fallback + local YOLO until retrain, or must specific foods be guaranteed for demo date?
9. Is plate quantity (how much eaten) required for launch, or is “what food was present” enough?

Alerts & notifications
----------------------
10. Which alerts must be real-time on screen only vs email/SMS/push to caregivers?
11. Cough severity thresholds: tune for nursery ambient noise, or per-room calibration?
12. Child-not-in-frame alert: required for every session or optional / configurable?

Dashboard & reporting (Phase 7 polish)
--------------------------------------
13. Required exports: CSV, PDF care report, or dashboard on-screen only?
14. How long should meal/allergen logs be retained (30 days, 1 year, until child ages out)?
15. Do regulators or parents need audit trails beyond current allergen_logs + food_diary?

Privacy & compliance
--------------------
16. Jurisdiction (UK GDPR, US state rules, healthcare vs consumer wellness)?
17. Where may video/audio be processed — UK/EU server only, or US cloud APIs (Clarifai, Azure) acceptable?
18. Is storing meal snapshots or video recordings in scope, or metadata + labels only?

Hardware & performance
----------------------
19. Target cameras: browser webcam, ManyCam virtual, fixed T40, or mixed?
20. Is the current Ubuntu VPS spec final, or will a GPU or edge device be added later?
21. Acceptable latency for food label and emotion on server (e.g. 2–3 s OK vs must feel instant)?

Launch scope
------------
22. MVP launch features: which phases are must-have vs post-launch (settings page, emotional history, quantity)?
23. Demo date and audience — nursery staff pilot, investor demo, or parent beta?
24. Who owns ongoing Clarifai / Azure API costs and monthly budget cap?
